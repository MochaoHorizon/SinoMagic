=====你知道吗  Do you know these ?=====
本模组项目正式启动于2026.2.16，
但是有关这个模组的设想在DMHR114514在游玩基岩版时就已经初步形成，并部分落实。
This mod project started on February 16th 2026,
but the idea about this mod was initially formed and partially implemented when DMHR114514 was playing the bedrock version.

鸡的三个空闲音效是DMHR114514在他家录的真实母鸡叫声，
走地鸡彩蛋音效（yaaah）则由Cloud1232534引用自网络。
Three spare sound of chicken are the real hen sound recorded by DMHR114514 in his home,
the special sound of walking chicken (yaaah) is quoted from the network by Cloud1232534.

其实DMHR114514最开始并不想先做豆制品，
但和网友们聊天的时候，最先形成思路的却是豆制品。
In fact, DMHR114514 didn't want to make soy products first,
but when chatting with netizens, it was soy products that first formed ideas.
